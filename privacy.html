Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains what information Alphoptic collects and how it's used. Alphoptic is a small, independently run project, and this policy is written in plain language rather than dense legal text — if anything is unclear, reach out via the Contact page.

Information collected automatically

Alphoptic uses Google Analytics to understand overall site traffic — things like which pages are visited, how long people stay, and general geographic region. This data is aggregated and anonymized by Google's tools; it is not used to identify individual visitors.

Information you provide directly

If you use the Contact form, the information you submit (name, email, reason, and message) is sent to a private Google Sheet for the purpose of responding to your inquiry. This information is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for marketing.

Payments

Optional support/tip payments are processed entirely through Stripe. Alphoptic does not receive or store your payment card details — that information is handled directly by Stripe under its own privacy policy.

Cookies

Google Analytics may set cookies in your browser to distinguish sessions and visits. You can disable cookies in your browser settings or use browser privacy extensions to opt out of analytics tracking.

Third-party data sources

  • Bill and legislative data is sourced from Congress.gov, a public government resource.
  • Congressional stock trade data is sourced from Financial Modeling Prep's public API.

Alphoptic does not control how these third parties handle data on their own platforms.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Alphoptic adds new features. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the Contact page.
