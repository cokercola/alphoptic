About Alphoptic

Alphoptic tracks bills moving through Congress and estimates how they might affect specific industries and publicly traded companies. It pulls bill data from Congress.gov, then uses AI to generate a plain-language read on each bill's likely direction and impact — who it helps, who it hurts, and how directly.

This is an independent, solo-built project — not affiliated with any government agency, financial institution, or news organization. It started as a way to make legislative activity easier to follow without wading through raw bill text, and it's still early: new bills, features, and data sources are added regularly.

What Alphoptic is not

Alphoptic is not financial advice, legal advice, or a substitute for reading actual bill text and consulting qualified professionals before making decisions based on legislative activity. Passage probabilities, impact scores, and direction assessments are AI-generated estimates, not predictions or guarantees.
